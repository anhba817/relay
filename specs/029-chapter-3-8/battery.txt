T059 — THE DECISION
-------------------

THE TRANSPORT BECOMES ITS OWN CHAPTER. The limiter is 3.8; the email transport is
3.9; quotas move to 3.10 and the isolation gauntlet to 3.11.

The number drove it and the number is decisive. The transport's prose has to
carry the outbox pattern for a third time, Mailpit's justification against
constitution VII, resolving recipients at send time from a column chapter 3.6
denormalised for exactly this, the unaddressable branch, why FR-021 can only be
settled by what a server RECEIVED, and the per-row isolation finding — which is
the best thing the sabotage battery produced this chapter and cannot be told in
two sentences. At this chapter's density that is 1,500-2,200 words, taking a
combined chapter to roughly 6,300 and past anything the series has shipped.

Split, both halves land in range: 3.8 trimmed toward 4,000, and 3.9 at or just
above the 2,000 floor. That floor was the argument AGAINST splitting and it is
the only one; a 1,400-word chapter would be worse than a long one. The material
is not 1,400 words.

R10 recommended the split when the chapter was planned, on an estimate. The scope
decision said one chapter. The phase order put the transport LAST precisely so
this could be decided with a measurement instead — and the measurement agrees
with R10, which is the first time an R10-style size warning in this series has
been confirmed rather than absorbed.

AND 3.9 IS WRITTEN IN THIS CYCLE, not deferred to its own. The alternative was to
ship the transport's code now and let a later chapter explain it, which would mean
about a dozen files entering `fences/post-series.md` — a file whose whole purpose
is small amendments no chapter teaches, not a subsystem. Writing 3.9 now keeps
every fence with the chapter that teaches it and leaves nothing orphaned. It costs
a second page, a second figures file, a second translation and a second registry
entry.

WHAT DOES NOT CHANGE: the code. Phase 8 shipped the transport and it closes
FR-WHK-07 whichever chapter explains it. `delivered_at` is set, chapter 3.6's
backlog drains, Mailpit is in `compose.yaml`, and the tag `part3-ch8` covers all
of it. The split moves prose, not commits.

THE RENUMBERING COST: quotas moves for the third time in three chapters. T054
measured what that costs now — zero fence amendments — because chapter 3.7's rule
stopped source comments citing chapter numbers. That rule is what makes this
decision cheap enough to make on the evidence rather than on inertia.
