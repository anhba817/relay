T008 — `quantile` AT THE SIZES A PER-TENANT-PER-HOUR BUCKET ACTUALLY HAS
    n        quantile(0.95)   quantileExact(0.95)    error
       4            3.85              4            3.7500%
      10            9.55             10            4.5000%
      25           23.80             24            0.8333%
      50           47.55             48            0.9375%
     100           95.05             96            0.9896%
     200          190.05            191            0.4974%
     500          475.05            476            0.1996%

  **R2 measured 0.9896% at n=100 and called that the worst. The real worst in this range is
  4.50%, at n=10** — and the median tenant holds ten rows in total, let alone per hour. The
  error does not fall monotonically with size either: 3.75% at four samples, 4.50% at ten,
  0.83% at twenty-five. `quantileExact` is the decision and this table is why.

T001a/T001b/T001c/T001d — CI, AND THE CORRECTION PASS 12 MADE TO PASS 9
  All three jobs read. The `platform` job — which runs `pnpm test:integration`, `pnpm coverage`
  and the error-registry gate — provided `postgres:18-alpine`, `redis:8-alpine`,
  `nats:2.12-alpine` and **no ClickHouse**. The `outsider` job runs `docker compose up -d
  --wait`, and compose's `clickhouse` carries no `profiles:` key, so it starts there. **Pass 9
  published "CI has no ClickHouse" from a grep of `ci.yml`; the word lives in `compose.yaml`.**
  A grep for a word is not a check for a capability.

  Applied:
    + a `clickhouse/clickhouse-server:25.3` service on the `platform` job, credentials and
      `CLICKHOUSE_DB` matching `compose.yaml`, health-checked with a real query rather than
      `/ping` (chapter 4.2's lesson)
    + `RELAY_CLICKHOUSE_HOST: localhost` in the job env
    + `node analytics/apply.mjs` after the Postgres migration — `CLICKHOUSE_DB` makes the
      database and not its tables
    ~ `check-error-codes.mjs` moved ABOVE `pnpm test:integration`. It used to close the job,
      after a step that has been red since 4.4 and carries no `continue-on-error`, so the gate
      that compares `ERROR_CODES` against the published reference **never executed**
    ~ `pnpm coverage` gained `if: always()`, for the same reason

  **WHAT CI STILL CANNOT DO (T001d).** `pnpm test:integration` is
  `turbo run … --concurrency=1` and stops scheduling at the first failure: it plans 18 tasks,
  attempts 9, and prints `Tasks: 7 successful, 9 total`. One red suite still hides every lane
  ordered after it, in CI exactly as locally (`gaps.md` 051-3). Provisioning the store does not
  fix that and this line exists so nobody reads it as fixed.
