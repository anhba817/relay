  AND THE MESSAGE FRAME IS NOT IN THAT LIST AT ALL. `message.created` reaches a client
  as a payload FORWARDED from the api, so no `channel:` literal in the gateway writes
  it. A design that edits 21 sites would miss it; a wrapper at the single `send`
  (session.ts:119) catches it. Phase 1 has therefore answered T012a with a
  measurement rather than a preference.
