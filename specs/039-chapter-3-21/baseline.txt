**C2 — THE PLANNED RATE LIMIT COULD NOT WORK, AND THE FIX MADE THE DESIGN
SMALLER.**
Research R5 said a third `"typing"` operation on the existing limiter needs "no

    limits.ts:89   const WINDOW_MS = 60_000
    limits.ts:115  `rl:${environmentId}:${operation}:${windowStartFor(now, WINDOW_MS)}`

    scope    per tenant   vs  per connection — 10,000 users would share one budget
                              and one chatty user would silence the rest
    window   60 s         vs  2 s — "N per minute" cannot express "one per 2 s"
    subject  no channel   vs  per channel

Three mismatches, each fatal alone. **The replacement is a `Map` on the
connection** — no Redis, no third operation, `limits.ts` not edited.

**And the per-environment ceiling was dropped rather than kept alongside.** It
would bound a rate the debounce already bounds: the gateway enforces the interval
itself, so a hostile client cannot exceed it by trying. What stays unbounded is the
number of connections, and that is FR-RTM-09's cap — chapter 3.22.

**The design got smaller because a premise was checked, not because somebody
wanted a smaller design.** Constitution VII passed the third bucket as a small
addition; it was an addition that could not do the job.
