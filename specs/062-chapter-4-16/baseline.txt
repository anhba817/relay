T012b **THE CATCH-ALL `else` WAS A REAL DEFECT AND THE COMPILER PROVED IT.** The moment
      the fourth arm existed, `tsc` said:

        ingest.ts(111,27): Argument of type 'ConnectionRow | MediaStoredRow' is not
        assignable to parameter of type 'ConnectionRow'.

      `else connections.push(routed.row)` was about to put media rows into
      `connection_events`. **It caught this one because the row shapes differ** — two
      record types that happened to be structurally compatible would have passed
      silently. Replaced with an exhaustive `switch` and a `never`, which is what 4.14 did
      to `session.ts` for the same reason.

T013/T014/T015/T016  THREE MIGRATIONS APPLIED, REAL EXIT 0 — `applied 3, skipped 16`.
      The applier keys on `(filename, checksum)` and refuses a changed file, unlike the
      Postgres runner that skips one in silence; and it strips comments before splitting
      on `;`, which is why three semicolons in prose are still one statement.

T017  **THE DESIGN WORKS END TO END, AND THE ARITHMETIC IS THE PROOF.** Two separate
