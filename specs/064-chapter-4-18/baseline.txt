T005  THE ROUTER SAYS 47, AND THE PARSE WAS RIGHT WHERE THE GREP WAS WRONG
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

      `targets.itest.ts`, 9 of 9, under `--reporter=verbose`:

        gauntlet targets: 47 derived, 39 attacked, 8 exempt
                          (read 6, write 26, credential 4, list 3, exempt 8)

      **Research R3 published two estimates and said only the router could settle it.**
      A regex over `targets.ts` found **47**; `grep -c "method:"` found **51**, and R3
      recorded the disagreement rather than picking. The router says 47 — so the parse
      was right and the grep was counting the string `method:` four times more often
      than there are entries. *A count taken from the text is a guess about the
      derivation*, and this is the case where one guess was exact and the other was not.

      **AND THE BOTH-DIRECTIONS ASSERTION IS WHAT LICENSES T010 TO USE THE FILE.** The
      suite passes only when every derived target matches an entry and every entry
      matches a derived target, so `targets.ts`'s list and the router's are the same
      list — proven this run, not assumed. T010 builds `routes.md` from the file with
      that warrant rather than from a fresh parse.
