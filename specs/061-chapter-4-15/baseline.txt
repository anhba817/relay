T017  `unreferencedMediaIn(db, environmentId, olderThan, limit)` — FR-MED-10's predicate.

      **A RENDITION IS NEVER RETURNED, AND THAT IS THE WHOLE OF FR-002.** The obvious
      reading — "a rendition is unreferenced when its parent is" — would have the reaper
      delete parent and rendition separately and depend on the order. It does not need
      to: `media_objects_parent_fk` is ON DELETE CASCADE, so the predicate asks only
      about uploads and the renditions follow. *"Its reachability is its parent's"* is
      discharged by the foreign key rather than by a second arm somebody keeps in step.

      **TWO QUERIES, NOT ONE, AND 4.12 MEASURED WHY.** The natural single statement puts
      `NOT EXISTS (… attachments @> … m.id …)` against each candidate, which builds the
      containment operand from a column on the other side of the join — a GIN index
      cannot be looked up with a value the planner does not have yet (1,042 buffers
      against 84). Candidates come back first; their ids go into the second query as
      bound values. **The first draft was THREE queries** — the middle one returned
      channels when what was needed was media ids — and was simplified before it ran.

T018  Its comment names the chapter that will call it (`docs/12` row 22, erasure) rather
      than claiming a caller it does not have. CLAUDE.md's convention, and the reason is

T020  THE DELIVERY GATE RESOLVES THE PARENT — in `repository.ts`'s

      draft sent it. One substitution: `object.parentId ?? mediaId` feeds the existing

T022  THE ATTACH PREDICATE REFUSES A RENDITION, AND IT WOULD HAVE PASSED WITHOUT IT.

      **FORCING THE INDEX MAKES IT WORSE.** 54 renditions among 6,646 rows, so a
      sequential scan beats an index scan and Postgres chooses correctly. This is 4.13's
      rule applied before writing an assertion — *"they asked whether Postgres CHOOSES an
      index, which is a property of the corpus"* — and the honest statement is that
      `media_objects_parent_idx` is the right shape for a population this lane does not
      have. T006 already said so from the other end: **4 of 5,996 images are above the
      bound**, so the lane cannot exercise this chapter at scale, and neither figure
      should be published as a claim about a real deployment.

T049/T050/T051  `rendition-delivery.itest.ts`, **7 of 7**. Every door, plus: the key is
      OMITTED rather than sent as null (asked with `Object.hasOwn`, because
      `toBeUndefined` also passes on `null`); a `pending` attachment offers nothing; and
      `mediaUpdatedSchema` REFUSES a thumbnail, because ADR-33's frame is a notification
