T009b — THE PLANTING DID NOT FAIL, BECAUSE THE CONSTRAINTS WERE READ FIRST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

T009b says to watch the planting fail once before trusting it, because research
hit `webhook_endpoints_disabled_check` while re-planting an endpoint by hand.

It did not fail. The four check constraints were read off the live schema before a
single `INSERT` was written:

  environments_kind_check              kind IN ('development','production')
  humans_provider_check                provider IN ('github','google')
  memberships_role_check               role IN ('owner','admin','member')
  webhook_endpoints_disabled_check     (disabled_at IS NULL) = (disabled_reason IS NULL)
  webhook_endpoints_failure_run_check  (failure_run_started_at IS NULL) = (failure_run_attempts IS NULL)

First run against a fresh database:

  {"endpoints":1,"due":200,"unpublished":200,"undelivered":200,"unaddressable":1}

Re-planted: due deliveries still 200, not 400. Idempotent (FR-003).

The task's instinct was right and its remedy was one step too late. "Watch it fail"
is what you do when you have guessed at a schema; reading `pg_constraint` first is
cheaper than a failure and produces the same knowledge. The five constraint
definitions are now comments beside the inserts they govern, which is where the
next person editing them will look.
