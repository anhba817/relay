THE METHOD, AND IT IS NOT "REGENERATE AGAINST THE TAG"

      The first repair measured what the obvious approach costs.
      `diff(chain state at 3.17, rework/part3-ch17)` for the coverage config is
      **206 changed lines in one hunk** — because the chain was already 148 lines
      behind ch16 before the chapter started, so the hunk absorbs the divergence
      and shows the reader 146 lines chapter 3.17 never wrote.

      What works instead: KEEP THE CHAPTER'S CHANGE AND TRIM THE CONTEXT THE
      CHAIN DOES NOT CARRY. Three strategies, in order:

        1. the published hunk, unchanged, if it anchors
        2. trimmed leading and trailing context, as far as it must go
        3. the interior gap — anchor on the longest leading and trailing runs
           that each occur once, and replace everything between them

      Strategy 3 is the one nothing had anticipated. It is needed when the lines
      the chain lacks sit in the MIDDLE of a pre-image, which is what a hunk that
      deletes a long block looks like when earlier chapters never added part of
      that block. `eslint.config.mjs` at 3.25 deletes 170 lines of which the
      chain has 154.

## THE APPLIER CORRUPTS `$$`, AND FR-016 WAS FILED AGAINST THE PROSE FOR IT

      The last problem refused to close. `sentinel.sql` kept reporting
      `chapters: "END $;"` against `repo: "END $$;"` however the fence was
      regenerated, and the fence plainly said `+END $$;`.

          node -e 'console.log("x".replace("x", "END $$;"))'   ->   END $;

      **`String.prototype.replace` reads `$$`, `$&`, `` $` ``, `$'` and `$<name>`
      in a STRING replacement as substitution patterns.** `applyHunks` used the
      string form, so a post-image containing `$$` landed as a single `$` — and
      `sentinel.sql` is `DO $$ … END $$;` twice over.

      SO FR-016's RECORDED DEFECT IS NOT IN THE PROSE. `gaps.md` and T066 say
      *"the published fence carries `END $;` where the repository has `END $$;` —
      a reader copying that listing gets a syntax error."* The fence carries
      `END $$;`. A reader copying it is fine. **The checker produced the `$;`, in
      its own replayed state, and two features filed it against the chapter.**

      Fixed with a function replacement, which is not subject to substitution:

          text = text.replace(p, () => post.join("\n"));

      **IT WAS CAUSING APPLY FAILURES TOO.** With the applier corrected and the
      ORIGINAL published hunk restored — the one phase 4 had rewritten — chapter
      3.23's `sentinel.sql` fence applies untouched. Two of the 42 "bad hunks"
      were never bad.

      AND THE REPAIR HAD BEEN COMPOUNDING THE BUG. Three rounds of regenerating
      against a corrupted state stacked three dollars: the chapter fence reached
      `+DO $$$` and the appendix `+DO $$$$`. Each round "fixed" the corruption by
      adding one more `$`, and each round the applier ate it again. Both fences
      were reverted to their pre-055 state and the file needed only the 19-line
      append the tree actually calls for.

      **A repair that keeps almost working is the shape of an instrument bug.**
