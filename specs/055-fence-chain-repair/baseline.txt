T011  IT CHANGES NO VERDICT, AND IT IS THE SAME REPLAY

        without the flag          110 problem(s) — APPLY 74, HEAD 36
        --dump X                  110 problem(s) — APPLY 74, HEAD 36
        -- --dump X               110 problem(s) — APPLY 74, HEAD 36
        two dumps, diff -r        byte-identical, including across the `--` form

        `--at` runs a SEPARATE replay with its own throwaway problem list, so the
        dump cannot add to, remove from or reorder what the check reports.

T011  THE POSITIVE CONTROL, BECAUSE DETERMINISM IS NOT CORRECTNESS

        A dump that is consistently wrong passes a dump-twice check. So: a hunk
        that works TODAY, against both modes.

        control   app/(en)/part-3/chapter-14/…/page.mdx:1252
                  a `diff` for services/api/src/users/users.controller.ts
                  first hunk pre-image, 21 lines

            --at chapter 3.14      occurs 1 time    APPLIES
            plain --dump           occurs 0 times   does not apply

        The two modes are different states, and `--at` stops BEFORE the page's
        own fences. Neither fact follows from determinism.
