T014 — `0 / 0` WAS NOT IN THE DESIGN, AND IT IS REACHABLE
  `data-model.md` gave the formula `abs(a - o) / max(a, o)` and the verdict ordering, and
  never said what two present zeroes mean. They are reachable: `usage_periods` holds 288 rows
  for 2026-08 with `messages_sent = 0`, and a rollup row can hold 0 for the same quantity.
  **They agree** — the guard returns 0 rather than NaN, and a test drives it.

T018 — AND THE `max(a, o)` DENOMINATOR MAKES THE THRESHOLD ASYMMETRIC
  The first boundary test asserted 100,000 against 100,100 as *"exactly 0.1%"* — the
  arithmetic anybody does in their head, 0.1% of 100,000 being 100. **It measures 0.0999%**,
  because the divisor is the larger side:

      100 / 100,100 = 0.000999000999…      just INSIDE the bound
      100 / 100,000 = 0.001                exactly ON it

  So a 0.1% shortfall and a 0.1% excess are different absolute amounts, and **a drift computed
  off the smaller side lands inside the threshold and does not fire**. That is the mistake
  phase 4's planted drift will make if nobody says so, which is why the test asserts both the
  correct pair (99,900 / 100,000) and the trap.

  Neither of these was visible in the design; both came from running the arithmetic.
