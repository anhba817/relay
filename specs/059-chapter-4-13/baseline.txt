THE LANE'S PENDING BACKLOG IS LONGER THAN ANY BATCH, AND THE FIRST TESTS DID NOT KNOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    media_objects where state = 'pending'   3,292
    oldest                                  2026-09-19 01:41:58+00     seven days
    the route's cap                         200 rows

`GET /internal/media/pending` is oldest-first, which is FR-009's anti-starvation property
and is the right order. It also means **an object created a millisecond ago is row 3,293
of a 200-row page**, so the first version of two tests asserted `toContain` against a list
that could never hold their fixtures — a test failing for the lane's reason rather than
the route's. The fixture backdates its own rows' `created_at` and `afterAll` deletes every
row its two environments own, scoped, so the next run's head is not this run's leftovers.

**The operational half is worth more than the test fix**: on a store with a real backlog a
fresh upload waits behind it, and the sweep has no notion of priority. Filed for the
record rather than solved here — a cursor or a claim is what a worker pool would need
(`data-model.md` §7a).

T027 — THREE ROUND TRIPS, AND WHETHER THEY COLLAPSE. THEY DO NOT, AND THE
       BREAK-EVEN IS 39%
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Against MinIO, 100 samples a side, one 10 MB object:

    HEAD                            p50 1.215   p95 2.049
    GET Range: bytes=0-65535        p50 2.627   p95 3.008    206 · bytes 0-65535/10485760
    GET whole (10 MB)               p50 8.853   p95 17.687
    HEAD, absent key                p50 1.167   p95 1.763
    GET Range, absent key           p50 1.943   p95 2.150

**A RANGED GET CARRIES EVERYTHING THE HEAD DOES.** Measured on the 206:
`content-range: bytes 0-65535/1048576` gives the total size, `last-modified` gives
SC-006's start instant, `content-type` gives the client's echoed claim. So the two
requests **can** be one, and the question is whether they should be.

    two trips   0.916 × 1.167  +  0.084 × 3.842  =  1.392 ms an object
    one trip    0.916 × 1.943  +  0.084 × 2.627  =  2.001 ms an object

**The two-trip design is 30% cheaper at this lane's mix and the ranged GET wins above
39% present.** The whole argument is that 91.6% of `pending` rows hold nothing, and for
those a `HEAD` is 40% cheaper than a ranged `GET` that has to build and discard an error
body. Above 39% the arithmetic inverts and one trip is right. **Neither is the better
design; the fraction decides, and nothing in this platform had ever measured it.**

T028 — PEAK RSS, AND THE FIRST MEASUREMENT WAS CONFOUNDED BY ITS OWN FIXTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    20 probes of a 100 MB object    baseline 46.2 MB   peak  80.7 MB   delta  34.5 MB

**1.4× THE OBJECT TO BUFFER IT**, because `arrayBuffer()` copies. That is the figure

T023b — THE MP4 PAIR, DECIDED AGAINST ITSELF
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

`audio/mp4` and `video/mp4` are one container. The brand at offset 8 is conclusive for
`M4A ` and `M4B ` and is used for both in `mp42`, `isom`, `avc1` and `iso5`; the
authoritative discriminator is an `hdlr` inside `moov`, which most encoders put at the
END of the file — past any prefix. **So the pair is treated as interchangeable and the
chapter says it cannot tell them apart**, rather than shipping a test that asserts a
distinction the bytes cannot make.

**AND THE CAPS STILL HOLD, WHICH IS WHY THE AMBIGUITY IS AFFORDABLE.** Declaring
`audio/mp4` and uploading a video buys the STRICTER cap — 25 MB rather than 100 — so the
pair cannot smuggle a large file past `KIND_CAPS`. What it permits is a video filed as
audio: a wrong label and no extra bytes.

T024 AND T023a — RED PER FORMAT, NOT ONCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Each reader arm deleted in turn and the suite re-run:

    dimensions   png   1 red     sniff   PNG   1 red      OggS       1 red
                 gif   2 red             JPEG  1 red      ID3        2 red
                 webp  1 red             GIF   1 red      frame sync 2 red
                 jpeg  2 red             WEBP  2 red      ftyp       3 red
                                         WAVE  2 red      declared===detected  1 red

**Every arm is separately covered, and two pairs alias.** `RIFF` opens both a WebP and a
WAV, so deleting either turns the other's discrimination test red as well — which is the
evidence that the byte-8 check is load-bearing rather than decoration. MP3 has two legal
starts and a reader knowing only `ID3` answers `null` for every stripped file; **`null` is
not a rejection**, so that failure would have been silent: the type check would simply
never have run.

**AND THE FIXTURES' CIRCULARITY IS NAMED.** A test that writes 640×400 into a header and
asserts the reader answers 640×400 has proved two pieces of code agree about an offset.
PNG gets a second, independent derivation — its IDAT inflates to exactly `height` rows of
`1 + width` bytes, so counting them recovers both numbers from bytes the header does not
contain. GIF's LZW, JPEG's entropy-coded scan and WebP's VP8L bitstream each need a
decoder to do the same, and the limit is written in the test file rather than papered
over.

T031 — "MATERIALLY LARGER" WAS UNQUANTIFIED, AND THE QUOTA SETTLED IT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FR-MED-03 says *"contradict their declaration"*. The acceptance scenario and T031 both
said *"materially"* and nothing gave a tolerance. `reserveMediaSlot` sums
`declared_bytes` (SRS 1.17), so **under any tolerance a client that under-declares is
billed for the declaration and stores the difference** — the tolerance would be a
storage discount with no clause behind it.

Exact comparison makes the quota correct by construction, and the test that says so is
**one byte**, in both directions. A five-thousand-byte mismatch is rejected under a
tolerance too, so a suite testing only the large case would be green against the design
this one refuses.
