fifth service enters it the moment its `main.ts` exists, with no build and no
`package.json`. **050 paid two chapters for that entry arriving late**, and turbo's cache
hid the red.

nothing starts it but a test suite spawning a child (`gaps.md` 050-8, open since 4.5).
