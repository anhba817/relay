## THE e2e FAILURE, DIAGNOSED — AND `gaps.md` C6 ANSWERED IN ONE FILE OF TEN

Chapter 3.23 recorded two battery failures it could not explain: an api child gone mid-run,
`ECONNREFUSED` inside the suite's own port range, and "`EADDRINUSE` greps to zero and **that
zero proves nothing** — such a line would sit in an unread pipe." This chapter read the pipe.

    service output; child output follows:
    --- api ---
    Error: listen EADDRINUSE: address already in use :::4100
        at Server.setupListenHandle [as _listen2] (node:net:1941:16)
        ...
      code: 'EADDRINUSE', errno: -98, syscall: 'listen', address: '::', port: 4100

**The `ECONNREFUSED` a test reports is the SECOND error.** The child died binding a port
another child still holds, the health check then found nothing listening, and only the second
error ever reached a human. 3.23 guessed exactly this and could not prove it.

**Why it was readable here and nowhere else.** `packages/e2e/src/harness.ts` does what C6's nine
files do not: `capture()` retains every line, `dump()` formats the last twelve per child, and
`serviceOutput()` exposes it. Of the three e2e files, **exactly one calls it** —
`tuan.itest.ts:60`, wrapping its own failure. The other two, `webhooks.itest.ts` and
`quotas.itest.ts`, fail with `ECONNREFUSED` and nothing else.

    the capture exists          harness.ts:342
    the formatter exists        harness.ts:352
    it is offered to callers    harness.ts:486  serviceOutput()
    callers that use it         ONE, tuan.itest.ts:60

So C6 is not "nobody captures the output" — it is **"the output is captured, formatted, offered,
and one caller in ten takes it."** That is a smaller and much cheaper gap than five chapters of
`gaps.md` have assumed, and the fix is a `catch` in two files.

### AND THE CAUSE IS THE FIXED PORT, WHICH IS C2

`harness.ts:411` is `Number(process.env.RELAY_E2E_API_PORT ?? 4100)` and the lane sets no such
variable. All three e2e files boot a harness on **4100/4101/4102**, `fileParallelism: false`
serialises the files, and a previous file's api has not always released 4100 by the time the
next one binds it. Two orphaned api children were still listening on 4999 and 4907 after my
runs, which is the same non-reaping in a different range.

### WHAT I GOT WRONG ON THE WAY, AND IT MATTERS

I ran the e2e package once at HEAD and once with phase 3, saw 10 passed against 1 failed, and
called it **decisive**. It was one run per arm. Then:

    with phase 3   6 runs, 6 failed — on THREE DIFFERENT FILES
                   tuan, webhooks, quotas, tuan, webhooks, tuan
    at HEAD        run 1 FAILED too (1 failed | 9 skipped)

**A cause does not move between files, and HEAD is not clean either.** The correct reading is a
port collision whose victim is whichever file runs when the previous child has not let go —
present before this chapter and unrelated to attachments. My "decisive" was the same error
`CLAUDE.md` warns about in its second paragraph, committed against n=1 per arm while quoting the
rule about confidence intervals in the same session.
