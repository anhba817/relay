FENCE BUDGET
------------

The plan set no fence budget — 3.5 and 3.6 both had one because both were
building subsystems and the count was the thing at risk. Shipped 9, and every
one is a diff rather than a whole file, because this chapter changes existing
code and adds none.

The 9 are: `resume.itest.ts` (the fourth-quadrant test and two more);
`resume.ts` (`suppressed`, `scopeMarks`); `registry.ts` (the `marks` field);
`session.ts` (three call sites and the rewritten comment); `schema.ts` and
`webhook-walk.mjs` (the chapter-number comments); `resume.test.ts` and
`session.test.ts` (the unit tests); `vitest.coverage.config.mts` (the ratchet).

One fence is not a file and is excluded from the count of nine: the `(excerpt)`
showing the gateway's two `await`s, which the chain checker skips by design. It
is the only fence in the chapter that is not byte-generated from the repository,
and it is an excerpt because the two statements it puts next to each other are
twenty lines apart in the real file (`session.ts:404` and `:424`), separated by
the ack and nineteen lines of comment about ordering — which is most of why
nobody saw them as two instants.

It also carried a method name that does not exist: `api.send` where the real call
is `api.sendMessage`. Caught while checking the twenty-line claim, which is an
argument for checking claims that sound like decoration.

SCHEMA.TS AND WEBHOOK-WALK.MJS ARE THE REASON FOR T035. Both chains end in
chapter 3.6, so their HEAD check fails the moment this chapter's commit lands
and stays failing until some chapter shows the amendment. They are fenced here
rather than in `fences/post-series.md` because this chapter has a section about
exactly why those comments were fragile — the rule being that a published
chapter may only carry a fence for a change it discusses.
