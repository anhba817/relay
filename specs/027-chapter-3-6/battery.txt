This chapter is 34% over the bound and chapter 3.5 was 25% over. Neither is
caught by any gate — `check:docs` passes, and the bound is stated in the plan
rather than enforced by a script. 3.5's own battery recorded a raw word count of
37,946 that includes fence bodies, which is a different measure and does not
answer the question the bound asks.

NOT SILENTLY ACCEPTED, and not unilaterally fixed either. Three honest options,
and the choice is a scope decision rather than an implementation one:

  * trim ~1,350 words. The candidates are the two longest Trap boxes and the
    sabotage section, which could state the findings without re-deriving them;
  * split the chapter at "Now the decision", which is a real seam — everything
    before it is the attempt record and everything after is auto-disable, and the
    chapter argues they are separable in exactly that place;
  * amend the bound in docs/07, which is the option that should be hardest,
    because a rule rewritten to fit the work it failed to bound is not a rule.

Recorded here rather than decided here. What the chapter should NOT do is ship
over the bound with nobody having noticed, which is the state 3.5 is in.

TWO HITS, both examined and both benign:

  * `whsec_do_not_publish_this`, four times in the published pages. It is the
    fake secret inside `analytics.test.ts`, attached to an attempt record by a
    test whose entire purpose is to assert that it does NOT reach the stream. A
    scanner finding it is the scanner working; a chapter that removed it would be
    deleting the evidence.
  * three 32-character hex strings in `captured-output.md`. They are the md5sums
    the sabotage battery prints to prove each mutated file was restored
    byte-identical.

No signing secret, no encryption key, no internal credential, and no bearer token
appears in any transcript or on either page. The one real secret this chapter
handles — a customer's webhook signing secret — is decrypted only inside the
dispatch material response, and the attempt record's allow-list is what keeps it
off the analytics stream.
