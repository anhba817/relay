## Two files the chapter asserts and does not fence — a defect

  packages/protocol/src/internal.test.ts    "Five unit cases ... hold the grammar"
  services/api/src/consumer/runtime.test.ts "two are pure and live in the unit lane"

Both exist in the repository and neither is fenced, so the fence chain cannot
replay them. The chapter's own checkpoint promises 133 unit tests, which is only
reachable if both files exist — so the prose asserts an artifact the fences do
not carry. Recorded here and in chapter-notes.md finding 1; spec FR-025 was added
in reconstruction to name the rule this breaks. Fixing it is a fence-only
amendment to a published chapter, in both locales.
