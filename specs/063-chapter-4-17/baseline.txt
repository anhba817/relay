T009  THE VERDICT TIMING, RE-MEASURED WITH INDEPENDENT PHASE — never in a back-to-back
      loop, because a loop that waits for the thing it is timing synchronises with it.

        this run, 10 trials   min 1,861 · p50 3,993 · max 5,568 · mean 3,625 ms
        analysis pass 5       min 1,197 · p50 2,953 · max 5,840 · mean 3,055 ms
        the discarded loop    min 5,656 · p50 5,693 · max 6,123 ms

      **THE TWO HONEST SAMPLES DIFFER BY ABOUT A SECOND AT THE MEDIAN AND ARE THE SAME
      DISTRIBUTION.** A p50 taken from ten draws of a uniform [0, 5,000] has a standard
      error near 500 ms, so 2,953 and 3,993 are one distribution seen twice. **The thing to
      publish is the generator, not the percentile**: a 5,000 ms timer the upload lands in
      at a random point, plus the work.

      **AND THE RESIDUAL IS NOISIER THAN T035 ASSUMED.** "The minimum minus the smallest
      possible wait" gives 1,197 on one sample and 1,861 on another — with ten draws the
      expected smallest wait is about 455 ms, so the two mins differ by less than the
      sampling noise. The ~700 ms figure is an order of magnitude, not a measurement, and
      4.13's direct measurement of the scan is the better number for that quantity.

      The false sentence is gone from `packages/outsider/src/integrate.itest.ts`. What

      explaining it away survived two chapters.

      **AND THE CORRECTION IS ASSERTED, NOT WRITTEN.** This chapter's whole subject applied
      to its own first repair: a comment is not a test. The `pending` expectation stays and

      reason is right — the read lands inside the window — then waiting past the window
      must produce a verdict, and the two lines now say so together.

      **RUN RED, AND THE PROBE CORRECTED THE CLAIM I WAS ABOUT TO MAKE FOR IT.** With
      `docker compose stop media-worker` the suite is 18 of 19, the `pending` line still
